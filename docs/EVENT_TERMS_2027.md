# Event terms for 2027

Reference copy of the terms participants accept when registering for the 2027 event. They're the
2026 terms plus three additions for participants under 18:

- Clause 4 (Consent to Monitoring): "This applies to all participants, including those under 18."
- Clause 5 (Limitation of Liability): "Nothing in these terms limits any liability that can't be
  limited by law, including for participants under 18."
- New clause 7 (Participants under 18): a parent or legal guardian who accepts the terms for a
  participant under 18 accepts them on their behalf and is responsible for their participation.

## Using them

1. Admin > Events > the 2027 event: paste the terms below into the terms field.
2. Set the terms version (e.g. `2027-v1`). Each acceptance, and each parent/guardian consent for a
   participant under 18, records the version it was given under, so change the version whenever the
   wording changes.
3. Check the confirm page and the guardian consent page show them as expected.

Before using them, have counsel confirm clause 5 and the guardian's acceptance under the law that
applies to the participants under 18 (typically Maryland).
